Analysing bit-planes…

Reconstruction from the top k planes

Keep the k most-significant planes and fill in the missing bits. Midpoint sets them to the centre of their range, (2n−k − 1)/2 (e.g. 128·B7 + 63.5, the constant baseline); random draws them uniformly from that range (a sample from a uniform prior, the no-model counterpart of a generative reconstruction); zero truncates them. Tiles show the midpoint fill.

Information per plane

H is the i.i.d. entropy (ignores structure). H | ctx is the entropy given the 4 causal neighbours (W, NW, N, NE) in the same plane — roughly what a context-model coder would pay. ρ↔ is the correlation with neighbouring pixels: near 1 for structured planes, near 0 for noise-like ones.
